Una Wang is a PhD candidate in Civil Engineering at ETH Zurich, where she researches machine agency, decentralized governance, and cyber-physical systems in the built environment. Her work bridges blockchain technology, autonomous systems, and regenerative systems, exploring how these technologies can transform ownership and governance in physical infrastructure. She has led research projects no1s1, co-founded initiatives such as DaoSuisse and Zuitzerland, and advises startups on blockchain integration and decentralized infrastructure.
